# Nancy AI Assistant

An Android app for blind and low-vision people, built by [Hemant Kumar](https://hemantkumar71002-del.github.io/), electronics engineer.

## What it does

- **Object detection:** the camera finds objects and speaks their direction, name and distance.
- **Face recognition:** recognises familiar faces, so you know who is in front of you.
- **Voice control:** everything works by voice, so no screen is needed.
- **Offline:** works without an internet connection.

Nancy AI is a helping tool. It does not replace a white cane or a guide.
